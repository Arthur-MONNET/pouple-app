What it does

Listens like Slack does

Mentions, direct messages, notes to yourself, group chats and the threads you take part in.

Investigates with Codex

Reads your projects read-only, finds the answer, and writes the short version. Nothing is changed before you approve.

Stays out of the way

Floating bubbles in a screen corner, a window for the full conversations, and a Stop button at any moment.

Ready in a few minutes

  1. Download and open PoupleDrag it to Applications. The first time, macOS may ask you to confirm: right-click the app, then Open.
  2. Connect SlackOne click creates the Slack app with the right permissions. Paste the token it gives you: Pouple shows whose account it is before saving.
  3. Connect CodexPouple installs the Codex CLI if needed and signs in with ChatGPT. It has its own login and never touches yours.

Your Slack token and every other key are kept in your Mac's keychain, never in a file or in the page. Pouple only talks to Slack and to Codex, from your Mac.
